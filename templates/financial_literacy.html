The Improving Financial Awareness & Financial Literacy Movement in Zambia

The Improving Financial Awareness & Financial Literacy Movement in Zambia™ is a collaborative initiative that seeks to improve access to financial education and empower individuals, families, young people, entrepreneurs and communities to make informed financial decisions.

JEF’s growing financial-awareness and financial-literacy work is strengthened through collaboration with The Financial Awareness Foundation (TFAF) and the Centre for Financial Literacy Education Africa (CFLE-Africa), with Mr. Prince Kapeya serving as Lead TFAF Ambassador - Zambia.

The work reflects a simple principle: financial strength is not determined by how much money someone has, but by how wisely they manage what they have.

Our vision is to build financially informed young people who become financially responsible individuals, sustainable entrepreneurs and active co-creators of Zambia’s development.

Our strategic direction

The movement aims to make financial education more accessible through public awareness campaigns, educational materials, university and college Financial Awareness Associations, trained student leaders, community engagement, youth networks, and collaboration with entrepreneurs and SMEs.

The movement is designed to complement existing financial literacy efforts in Zambia, build on what is already working, and encourage collaboration rather than duplication.

April Financial Literacy Month - 2026 Campaign Archive

Theme: Smart Money Habits for a Financially Confident Generation.

Financial Awareness Associations - Universities and Colleges

JEF is working towards establishing Financial Awareness Associations in universities and colleges. These associations will provide a platform for students to strengthen their financial knowledge, share practical learning with peers, and participate in financial awareness activities on campus and in their communities.

Proposed areas of activity include:

  • Student-led financial education and peer learning
  • Campus campaigns and financial awareness events
  • Student leadership and ambassador development
  • Mentorship and professional exposure opportunities, subject to available partnerships
  • Collaboration with university departments, student organisations and external institutions
  • Participation in April and October financial awareness campaigns

Universities and colleges interested in exploring an FA Association are invited to contact JEF through the partnership enquiry form.

Community, Youth and Entrepreneurship Engagement

JEF connects financial literacy to its wider youth empowerment mission. Financial education supports young people and entrepreneurs to make informed decisions, manage income, plan business activity, understand financial responsibilities and prepare for future needs.

Engagement may take place through community programmes, youth networks, schools, colleges, universities, entrepreneur and SME platforms, digital campaigns, public events and media.
