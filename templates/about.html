Who we are

The Jonuel Entrepreneurship Foundation (JEF) is a Zambian youth-led nonprofit organisation established in Mongu, Western Province. We are registered under PACRA as a Company by Guarantee.

JEF began as a direct response to growing youth unemployment, poverty and lack of economic opportunity in under-served communities of Western Province. Our mission is to transform the lives of youth, women and persons with disabilities through entrepreneurship, skills development, agriculture, governance, gender justice advocacy and innovation.

Since 2021, JEF has reached 6,710 youths and women through programmes that create awareness, support informed decision-making and promote hands-on skills.

We believe that even if we cannot change the whole world, we can change the environment around us, by nurturing empowered, self-reliant and community-driven young people who become the architects of their own futures.

Our Vision

A Zambia where every young person is skilled, productive and purpose-driven, transforming potential into impact and creating pathways for inclusive economic and social development.

Our Mission

To nurture a generation of empowered, ethical and innovative young Zambians through practical training, mentorship and institutional collaboration that bridges the gap between education, employment and enterprise creation.

Our values

Responsibility

We are committed to ethical practice, transparency and accountable leadership in all we do.

Transformative

We build generational impact by equipping young people with knowledge, resources and life opportunities.

Inclusive

We design programmes that centre the voices and needs of youth, women and persons with disabilities.

Our leadership

Mr. Chindele Chingumbe

Founder and Executive Director

Mr. Chindele Chingumbe is the founder of JEF. He is an International Youth Empowerment and Development Practitioner, a Mandela Washington Fellowship alumnus, a Board Member of the European Union Zambia Youth Sounding Board and a Gender Justice Activist. His experience at the Mandela Washington Fellowship inspired the creation of the YESDF Fellowship.

Why partner with JEF

  • Strong partnerships with communities, government and civil society organisations
  • A diverse and comprehensive programme portfolio
  • A dedicated youth-led leadership team
  • A unique focus on hands-on, practical projects
  • Personalised mentorship for every beneficiary
  • A government-endorsed fellowship programme (YESDF)

How we are sustained

JEF runs a sustainable nonprofit model that combines community impact with diverse support: grants from local and international development agencies, sponsorships and institutional partnerships, agriculture and fresh produce projects, programme registration fees, and community donations and fundraising.

Our partners

Government ministries and agencies; local schools, colleges and universities; international and local NGOs; youth networks and IT communities.
