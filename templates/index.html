About JEF

The Jonuel Entrepreneurship Foundation was founded in 2020 by Mr. Chindele Chingumbe as a direct response to youth unemployment, poverty and limited economic opportunity in Western Province.

We believe that even if we cannot change the whole world, we can change the environment around us, by nurturing self-reliant, community-driven young people who become the architects of their own futures.

Mr. Chindele ChingumbeFounder and Executive Director

Our vision

A Zambia where every young person is skilled, productive and purpose-driven, transforming potential into impact and creating pathways for inclusive economic and social development.

Our mission

To nurture a generation of empowered, ethical and innovative young Zambians through practical training, mentorship and institutional collaboration that bridges the gap between education, employment and enterprise creation.

Our five focus areas

Each area is grounded in community need and a commitment to inclusive development.

Skills and entrepreneurship

Practical training in agriculture, tailoring, digital technology and business, with mentorship and incubation support.

Governance and civic advocacy

Civic literacy, youth participation in decision-making and advocacy for accountable leadership.

Gender justice and SRH

Awareness campaigns and community dialogues on gender-based violence, early marriage and reproductive health.

Climate change and agriculture

Climate-smart farming, agribusiness and fresh produce skills for young agripreneurs.

Mental health support

Awareness, peer support and psychosocial tools that build emotional resilience among youth.

What we have done

Programs that have helped participants start farms, tailoring businesses and digital careers.

Agribusiness

Training in backyard gardening, poultry production and climate-smart agriculture.

Digital skills

Computing, Microsoft Office, graphic design, digital marketing and data entry.

Tailoring and fashion

Tailoring, cutting and fashion design, especially for young women.

Fairs and summits

Entrepreneurship skills fairs and youth summits connecting people with mentors and markets.

Leadership
